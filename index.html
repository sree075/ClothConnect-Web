ABOUT CLOTHCONNECT

One Platform. Many Helping Hands.

ClothConnect is a community-driven clothing donation platform that connects people who want to donate clothes with people who need them.

Instead of letting unused clothes sit inside wardrobes, ClothConnect gives them a second life while helping families in need.
